# Budweiser Racing League User Agreement

Version 2026-11-02.1 · Adults 18 and older

Required for every driver, owner, representative, recorder and administrator. No role or prior membership bypasses acceptance.

## 1. Parties, purpose and eligibility

This User Agreement is between you and the league administration operating the Budweiser Racing League application (BRL, the league, or we). It governs your use of the league website, application, account services and league participation tools. You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter an agreement in your jurisdiction. A parent, teammate, owner or administrator cannot accept for you. If you are under 18, do not register, use another person’s account, or submit personal information.

BRL is a community simulation-racing league. Names, teams, sponsor relationships, budgets and media features may represent fictional or game-based activity. Unless a separate authorized written statement expressly says otherwise, the app is not an official product of, endorsed by, or affiliated with Budweiser, Anheuser-Busch, NASCAR, Toyota, Ford, Chevrolet, a game publisher, a console platform, or any real broadcaster. Their names, marks, games and other materials belong to their respective rights holders.

## 2. Your affirmative acceptance and electronic records

Access requires your own authenticated account, confirmation that you are at least 18, your registered gamertag, and affirmative acceptance of this exact agreement version. Signing in, a preselected box, a cached browser setting or an administrator’s approval is not a substitute for your acceptance. Acceptance does not approve an ownership application, grant an administrative role, activate a race seat, reserve a number or sign a driver contract.

You agree to use electronic records and typed-gamertag acknowledgments for this agreement and league processes. A record may include your account identifier, registered gamertag, agreement version, the document fingerprint, acceptance time, adult attestation and applicable league roles. The recorded version is retained for accountability; later edits do not replace your earlier acceptance. You can view and print or save the agreement before and after accepting. You need a browser capable of displaying this page; do not accept if you cannot read or retain it.

You may decline and sign out. You may withdraw consent to future electronic use by contacting league administration through the established league communication channel and stopping use of the app; electronic league access will end unless an alternative is expressly arranged. Withdrawal does not undo completed actions or lawful retention of earlier records. Request a readable copy or discuss access problems with league administration before accepting if you need another format.

## 3. Accounts, truthful identity and security

Use your actual racing-platform gamertag as your displayed identity and signature. Keep registration, team and eligibility information accurate. Do not impersonate another driver, owner, administrator or organization; operate duplicate accounts to evade restrictions; share credentials; or use another member’s login. Requested roles and approved roles are different. Access is personal, limited and revocable under this agreement.

Choose a unique password and protect your device and recovery browser. Administrators should never ask you to send them your password. Password recovery requires the published verification process; do not approve a reset or access request for someone you have not verified. Report suspected compromise, incorrect permissions and accidental data exposure promptly. Do not exploit an error while waiting for a response. Stop using an account if you cannot reasonably keep it secure.

## 4. League rules and fair participation

Follow the current published season regulations, financial summary, event notices, game/platform rules and the requirements of your signed league agreements. Published schedules and deadlines use the league’s stated time zone; check daylight-saving changes rather than assuming a fixed UTC offset. Review official updates before each event. Do not use a schedule or interface error as permission to ignore a clearly published obligation.

Race fairly. Do not cheat, use unauthorized modifications, intentionally wreck or sabotage another competitor, manipulate results, coordinate prohibited voting, falsify evidence, exploit bugs or interfere with timing, scoring, inspections or administration. Participate under the registered identity and assigned number. Follow qualifying, race-start, attendance, start-and-park, substitute-driver, series-eligibility, field-size and late-entry rules. A registration or roster listing is not permission to race without the required approvals.

Required pre-race and post-race interviews, media responses, paint uploads, sponsor appearances and verification tasks must be truthful and completed by the published deadlines. Do not submit an AI-generated statement as an authentic quote, fabricate proof of participation, or claim a real person endorsed you. Report an absence, disconnection, technical problem or requested exemption using the designated process. An exemption must be reviewed; it is not automatic.

## 5. Conduct, communication and member safety

Treat members and administrators with respect, including in app messages, race communications and league-related channels. Racing banter does not excuse threats, stalking, discriminatory abuse, targeted harassment, doxxing, sexual harassment, nonconsensual intimate material, fraud or retaliation. Do not publish private messages, contact details, credentials, confidential contract information or another person’s sensitive information without a lawful basis and any necessary permission.

Do not spam, distribute malicious files, solicit passwords, promote unauthorized wagering, or use league services for unlawful activity. Submit disputes through the appeal process rather than threatening another member. If a situation involves immediate danger, use appropriate emergency services; this app is not an emergency service. Adults-only membership does not permit unlawful or exploitative material.

## 6. Driver responsibilities

A driver must apply through the approved process, obtain owner approval for the seat, and review and sign the current driver agreement before activation. Review compensation, payment dates, manufacturer, leased number, term, sponsor/media duties, bonus conditions, buyout, release and transfer provisions. Do not sign terms you do not understand or rely on an unsigned chat message as an activated contract. Each revised proposal requires the signatures required by that workflow.

Use only your assigned, team-authorized number and manufacturer. The league owns the number pool; the right to use a number is seasonal and subject to the approved lease and league redistribution process. A driver does not gain permanent ownership of a number or a right to sell it. Do not change teams, enter as a substitute or participate in another series in violation of the applicable approvals and eligibility rules.

Complete attendance, racecraft, interview, sponsor, manufacturer, scheme and evidence duties under the published rules and signed agreements. The base, franchise and champion roles have different required compensation. Do not falsely accept a lower role to evade a mandatory salary, submit a false signature or waive a required league minimum through an off-app arrangement. Compensation and deductions must follow the approved financial workflow. Use formal review for a disputed DNF charge, penalty, missed payment, buyout or release.

## 7. Owner and independent-owner responsibilities

Ownership and independent-team participation require the super administrator’s approval. Stay within the approved car count, charter, manufacturer and alliance limits. Do not advertise an unapproved charter, promise a number you have not secured, recruit into an unavailable seat, or make league-wide commitments on the league’s behalf. Independent owners remain responsible for the owner duties that apply to their configuration.

Before spending, read and acknowledge the team’s itemized salary and operating-cost breakdown. Keep required owner, base-driver, franchise and champion compensation funded and protected. Owners must take the required owner salary; designated franchise drivers and the defending champion must receive the applicable required minimums. Four-car teams require a franchise driver; three-car teams may choose one under the approved funding plan. A non-driving owner’s management pay is additional to the salaries for the actual driving seats.

Use the approved team-funding process. Returning-team operating grants, championship adjustments, new-team financing requirements and car-count scaling follow the season’s approved policy. A grant is not permission to double-claim earlier funding. Extra salary, signing bonuses, unusually high costs and other commitments above the funded minimums require available team income or approved financing. Pending claims and projected prizes are not cash. The starting operating reserve is not a guarantee that every future expense or next-season payroll is covered.

Lease each required number for the season, pay the published lease share, and comply with any extra manufacturer obligations accepted in exchange for assistance. Review and sign driver contracts honestly, maintain sufficient funds, and pay agreed compensation when due even if a sponsor or manufacturer claim is late, denied or unpaid. Submit genuine car-upload and race-use evidence. Do not pay an unverified claim, misrepresent sponsor coverage, divert protected payroll or route transactions through another account to bypass a restriction.

Borrow only through an approved loan agreement. Review the required income coverage, deadline, repayment schedule, automatic repayment share, late-charge rule and restrictions on overdue debt. Do not assume a grant, loan, lease, sponsor contract or charter renews automatically. Ownership changes, releases, transfers, insolvency and season renewal require league review; abandoning the team does not silently erase recorded obligations.

## 8. Administrator, recorder and manufacturer-representative duties

All administrators, including the retained super administrator, must personally accept this agreement. Authority comes from the assigned role and approved permissions, not a title, shared password or browser flag. Use the least access needed. A race recorder, manufacturer representative, owner and full administrator have different powers. Do not promote yourself, bypass ownership approval, lend an administrative account or use privileged data for an unrelated personal purpose.

Verify identities before password resets or sensitive changes. Keep credentials, service keys, recovery tickets and private member data confidential. Do not request a member’s password, disclose a private team budget to an unauthorized person, export member data for a competing service, or enter an acceptance or signature for another user. Correct errors through an audited process; do not erase or backdate contracts, approvals, votes, race results or financial history to conceal an action.

Apply rules consistently, inspect evidence, disclose conflicts of interest and seek another authorized reviewer where practical when you have a personal stake. Distinguish reported allegations from established findings. Do not retaliate against an appeal, fabricate evidence, favor your team through unauthorized grants, or issue a penalty outside your assigned authority. Review financial claims and number requests before authorizing payment. Keep an appropriate record of the decision and protect security information when explaining it.

Super-admin approval does not authorize unlawful conduct or override a member’s nonwaivable rights. Manufacturer representatives may act only within their appointment and may not imply they speak for a real vehicle manufacturer. Monitor unusual access and transactions, report suspected compromise and preserve necessary evidence. Use official role changes and account-recovery procedures rather than informal workarounds.
