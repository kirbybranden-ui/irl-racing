# Review notes for the league operator

This is a practical agreement draft and implemented access workflow, not a legal opinion or a guarantee of enforceability. Have qualified counsel review it before production publication, particularly the operator's legal identity and contact method, actual data practices/retention, brand and content permissions, electronic records, and applicable consumer/privacy rights. The draft does not invent a registered corporation, a mailing address, a jurisdiction, an arbitration mandate or real-money terms.

The league selected adults 18 and older. Age is an affirmative attestation, not verification against a government ID or date of birth. No parent-consent workflow is represented as implemented. Existing members, including administrators, are not grandfathered.

The document distinguishes virtual league credits from real currency. Any actual paid event, entry fee, employment relationship, prize, commercial sponsorship or gambling activity needs separate applicable terms and legal review. Do not advertise the app as officially endorsed by the real-world brands or broadcasters merely because simulation content uses their names.

Copying warnings cover protected expression, confidential information, misleading impersonation and access-control circumvention. They expressly preserve lawful independent development, facts and ideas, third-party licenses, fair use and other nonwaivable rights. The agreement cannot create ownership of third-party assets or make every imitation a crime. Check the source repository's actual licensing and permissions before sending a takedown or asserting ownership. No automatic civil fine or guaranteed statutory award is promised.

The versioned clickwrap records an authenticated account, registered gamertag, adult and consent attestations, agreement version/fingerprint, role snapshot and timestamp. It does not prove who was physically at a device and is not an identity-document verification service. It does not replace separate driver, owner, sponsor, loan or lease signatures.

The access gate protects league app routes, exposed database access and the patched media endpoint. Browser JavaScript, publicly hosted brand/game images, already-public GitHub source and public CDN/storage URLs cannot be made secret by this agreement or a client-side gate. Public-bucket object downloads may bypass Storage RLS. Private files must use appropriately private buckets, limited policies and short-lived signed URLs. Do not place credentials or confidential member data in public assets.
